# Card Fraud

## 1. Overview
**Card fraud** encompasses techniques used to steal payment data (card number, expiry date, CVV) and then carry out unauthorized transactions or clone cards.

## 2. How the Scam Works
1. **Data Collection**
   - Skimming, fraudulent payment sites, phishing, malware.
2. **Exploitation**
   - Online purchases, cash withdrawals, or reselling data on the dark web.
3. **Escalation**
   - Multiple rapid transactions or small test charges before larger purchases.

## 3. Key Red Flags
- Unusual or foreign transactions.
- Unexpected payment notifications.
- Payment declined then suddenly accepted after multiple attempts.

## 4. Prevention
- Use 3D Secure payment systems and tokenization.
- Never enter card details on unsecured (non-HTTPS) sites.
- Regularly monitor bank statements.
- Keep systems and anti-malware software up to date.

## 5. Response Steps
- Immediately contact the bank to block the card.
- Dispute fraudulent transactions and request a replacement card.
- Report the fraud to the relevant authorities.

## 6. Summary
Card fraud exploits stolen payment data. Technical protections and transactional vigilance are essential.
