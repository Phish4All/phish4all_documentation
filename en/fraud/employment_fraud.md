# Employment Fraud (Fake Recruiters)

## 1. Overview
**Employment fraud** covers scams where individuals posing as recruiters or employers offer fake jobs to obtain personal information, collect fees, or divert money.

## 2. How the Scam Works
1. **Initial Approach**
   - Offers via job boards, private messages, or emails.
2. **Information Requested**
   - Résumés, bank details, ID documents, and sometimes payment of registration or equipment fees.
3. **Exploitation**
   - Identity theft, fraudulent payments, fake reimbursement scams.

## 3. Key Red Flags
- Requests for payment to process a job application.
- Offers with abnormally high salaries and no real interview.
- Personal email addresses or non-professional domains.

## 4. Prevention
- Verify job offers and the company through independent sources.
- Never pay for a legitimate job offer.
- Check contact details and request a video or phone interview.

## 5. Response Steps
- Cut all communication and keep any correspondence.
- Report the listing to the job site and to the authorities.
- Check whether personal data has been compromised and act accordingly (credit monitoring, password changes).

## 6. Summary
Employment fraud exploits the need for work. Vigilance, verification, and wariness of financial requests protect job seekers.
