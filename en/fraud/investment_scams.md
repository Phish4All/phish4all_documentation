# Investment Scams

## 1. Overview
**Investment scams** are fraudulent schemes offering fake investment opportunities (stocks, cryptocurrencies, derivatives, etc.) designed to extract money from victims by promising high or guaranteed returns.

## 2. How the Scam Works
1. **Attraction**
   - Advertisements, social media messages, targeted emails promoting "opportunities."
2. **Luring**
   - Convincing sales pages, fabricated testimonials, demonstrations of gains.
3. **Initial Investment**
   - Victim transfers money to a platform or wallet controlled by the scammer.
4. **Escalation and Lock-out**
   - Demands for additional fees, inability to withdraw, platform disappears.

## 3. Key Red Flags
- Promises of high returns with little or no risk.
- Pressure to invest quickly ("limited-time offer").
- Unregulated platforms or those lacking clear legal information.
- Unrealistic testimonials with no independent verification.

## 4. Prevention
- Verify the regulation and licenses of the platform or broker.
- Conduct independent research and consult reliable sources.
- Do not give in to FOMO (fear of missing out) pressure.
- Start with small amounts and test withdrawals first.

## 5. Response Steps
- Stop any further payments and document all exchanges.
- Contact payment platforms and the bank to attempt to block transfers.
- Report the platform to financial authorities and anti-fraud services.

## 6. Summary
Investment scams exploit the lure of profit. Caution, regulatory verification, and financial education significantly reduce exposure.
